Delete your My Task account

Sign in below to permanently delete your account. You can also do this in the app: Profile → Delete account.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, phone number and password
  • Tasks you created
  • Your direct messages, notifications and accepted invites

What is kept

  • Tasks other people assigned to you stay with their creator; you are removed from them.
  • Messages you wrote on other people's tasks stay, shown as "Deleted user".

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. You can sign up again later with the same email as a new account.

Company accounts are managed by your organisation — please ask your administrator to delete them.
